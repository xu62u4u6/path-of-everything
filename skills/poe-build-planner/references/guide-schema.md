# Build Guide Schema

Use only sections that add decisions. Keep exact values sourced and dated.

## Header

- Patch, league, mode, platform if relevant.
- Research and price lookup date.
- Intended content and expected budget ceiling.

## Identity

- One-paragraph gameplay loop.
- Build strengths, weaknesses, buttons, ramp, and failure mode.
- Core interaction proof with links.

## Variants

Compare each meaningful route by:

| Variant | Entry gate | Damage shape | Defence | Mapping | Bossing | Cost | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Recommend one default and state why.

## Progression

Define milestones by functional transitions rather than arbitrary character levels:

1. Campaign skill and first resource solution.
2. Early maps with minimum links and defensive obligations.
3. Core engine activation.
4. Established setup with reliable state uptime.
5. High-investment scaling.

At each transition list prerequisites, parts changed together, and a rollback option.

## Gem Groups

For each stage provide main links in priority order, movement, auras, curses, guard skill, utility, minions, and optional swaps. Verify colors, item-provided or Imbued supports, attainable level/quality, modification limits, and current-league source.

## Passive Plan

Provide ascendancy order, masteries, keystones, jewel sockets, clusters, and respec points. Explain what each branch solves. Link a current tree or PoB when available.

## Gear Matrix

| Slot | Requirement | Entry | Established | High investment | Substitute/transition warning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

For rares, give affix priorities and feasibility rather than a fictional perfect item. For uniques, include current price, roll-sensitive mods, and whether a legacy or special variant is required. Label each required special component farmable now, trade-only finite supply, Standard-only, or unavailable.

## Minion Support Packages

When AG or spectres are considered, provide exact equipment or identities, effects, range, uptime, socket/reservation cost, survival investment, replacement risk, and a no-package fallback. Separate permanent proximity effects from temporary skills and conditional casts.

## State Assumptions

List permanent, sustainable after ramp, burst-only, mapping-only, and unsupported states. Include boss-opening and phase-transition behavior.

## Performance

Give ranges for baseline, realistic sustained, and burst damage/defence. State overlap/count assumptions and unsupported PoB calculations. Avoid promising content solely from DPS.

## Upgrade Queue

Order upgrades by dependency and expected effect. Compare complete legal before/after packages, including lost built-in supports, corruptions, sockets, and states. Include cheap fixes before luxury damage and warn when an upgrade breaks attributes, resistance, reservation, sockets, recovery, or charge generation.

## Content Notes

Cover map mods, ailment/curse/stun handling, recovery denial, reflect if relevant, no-add bosses, invitations, and death recovery.

## Unknowns

End with unverified mechanics, missing prices, hidden AG/spectre details, and the exact PoB experiments needed next.
